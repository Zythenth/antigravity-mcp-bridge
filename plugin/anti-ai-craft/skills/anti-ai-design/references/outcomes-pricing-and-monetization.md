## ADO-059 — Show the cost before material commitment

**Strength.** required

**Apply when.** A person compares plans, starts a contract, receives a quote, activates recurring billing, or chooses an option with a later cost.

**Decision.** Present amount, currency, billing interval, known taxes or fees, renewal terms, material limits, and the consequence of the choice before confirmation. Use direct language and keep this information accessible by keyboard, screen reader, and smaller devices.

**Evidence.** A person can explain how much they will pay now and later, what changes with each alternative, and where to review the contract without relying on support.

**Avoid.** A price that appears only at the end, an amount fragmented across secondary text, an unexplained calculated cost, a surprise renewal, or a comparison omitting a material difference.

## ADO-060 — Differentiate plans through comprehensible capabilities

**Strength.** default

**Apply when.** There are two or more offers, access levels, packages, licenses, or service options.

**Decision.** Organize alternatives by capabilities people recognize in their own tasks, with consistent differences and stable vocabulary. Highlight a recommendation only when there is an explicit criterion, and preserve full access to the other paths.

**Evidence.** The comparison makes it possible to identify which alternative meets each need, what remains available, and what will require a future change; people with different objectives arrive at justified choices.

**Avoid.** Promotional names without operational meaning, differences hidden in a tooltip, incomparable items in the same row, or a recommendation treated as the only valid option.

## ADO-061 — Tie limits to a real need

**Strength.** default

**Apply when.** A resource, volume, user, history period, export, or integration reaches a usage ceiling.

**Decision.** Communicate the limit in advance, explain the affected capability, and offer a proportionate action: adjust usage, review the plan, export, contact support, or continue with reduced functionality when safe. Connect the offer to the work the person is trying to complete, not to an arbitrary interruption.

**Evidence.** The limit is measurable, shown with a clear origin, and tested in near-limit, reached, and recovered states; the person understands the consequence without losing work or data.

**Avoid.** Sudden blocking, an obscure count, limiting a critical feature to create urgency, hiding a free alternative, or implying that a person's data will be lost when it remains recoverable.

## ADO-062 — Prioritize without opaque preselection

**Strength.** required

**Apply when.** An interface suggests an option, billing frequency, quantity, configuration, add-on, or renewal.

**Decision.** Use an initial preference only when it has a defensible justification for the person, and allow simple, visible, penalty-free changes. Explain a recommendation when it is based on data, and never turn a commercial choice into implied consent.

**Evidence.** The initial option, criterion, path to change, and consequence of each selection are verifiable without reading lengthy terms; tests include keyboard, touch, and assistive technology.

**Avoid.** A prechecked box for an additional charge, a less favorable option hidden from view, changing selection during the flow, confirming through ambiguity, or using fear of loss to prevent change.

## ADO-063 — Confirm payment with verifiable consent

**Strength.** required

**Apply when.** A person registers a payment method, starts a paid period, authorizes renewal, purchases an add-on, or increases recurring spend.

**Decision.** Separate review from confirmation, summarize the financial commitment in clear language, and present persistent confirmation after the action. Make failures, pending states, and the need for renewed authorization comprehensible without exposing sensitive financial data.

**Evidence.** The flow records the applicable authorization, shows a receipt or equivalent reference, and lets the person locate, review, and manage billing later.

**Avoid.** Billing by inertia, a button with a generic label, confirmation that disappears, requiring a call or chat to review payment, or blocking cancellation through an interface error.

## ADO-064 — Treat the trial as a reversible path

**Strength.** default

**Apply when.** The product provides temporary access, a demonstration, a trial period, or unlocked capability before a purchase decision.

**Decision.** Explain the start, duration, end criteria, available features, preserved data, and what happens if the person does not continue. Provide proportionate reminders and allow access to be ended or reduced through the same channel where it began.

**Evidence.** Someone can test real value, decide without surprise, and leave without losing exportable data or encountering additional barriers; expiration and recovery states were exercised.

**Avoid.** An artificial clock, hidden deadline, excessive reminders, turning a trial into a payment obligation, or making exit conditional on talking to sales when unnecessary.

## ADO-065 — Offer expansion in the context of need

**Strength.** heuristic

**Apply when.** A person demonstrates a recurring need for more capacity, collaboration, support, automation, or another service level.

**Decision.** Present the possibility of expansion near the relevant task, explaining the problem it solves, the cost, and available alternatives. Allow the offer to be dismissed and the task resumed without loss of focus or persistent repetition.

**Evidence.** There is evidence that the offer appears after a legitimate signal of need rather than at a random moment; tests confirm that it does not interrupt critical flows or reduce comprehension.

**Avoid.** A pop-up every session, an offer based on sensitive data, interruption during an error, a discount presented as a threat, or a commercial prompt that prevents use already purchased.

## ADO-066 — Make comparison auditable

**Strength.** default

**Apply when.** Plans, prices, benefits, limits, or terms are compared in a table, cards, calculator, or proposal.

**Decision.** Use the same criteria for every alternative, group differences by intent, and make details, terms, and exceptions available near the corresponding claim. Ensure reading remains viable with zoom, reflow, keyboard, and screen reader.

**Evidence.** The comparison can be reviewed without hidden calculation, each claim points to the applicable condition, and the interface preserves the relationship among row, plan, and consequence in responsive formats.

**Avoid.** Highlighting only favorable attributes, hiding restrictions behind a distant link, using color as the only distinction, converting a necessary table into ambiguous cards, or simulating savings without a verifiable basis.

## ADO-067 — Protect continuity, data, and portability between plans

**Strength.** required

**Apply when.** A person downgrades a plan, loses access to a capability, ends a trial, or changes billing options.

**Decision.** Explain precisely what remains accessible, what stops working, how long data remains available, and how to export or migrate content. Offer a reversible transition when technically possible, and do not create artificial dependency on format, history, or configuration.

**Evidence.** Tests cover downgrade, return, expiration, and export; exported files are useful, recovery documentation is discoverable, and permissions remain enforced.

**Avoid.** Deleting data as commercial pressure, making an export incomplete without warning, hiding the retention period, requiring a higher plan merely to recover one's own content, or preventing return through administrative friction.

## ADO-068 — Verify the commercial effect and possible harm

**Strength.** required

**Apply when.** A change to price, plan, limit, payment, offer, or commercial message is intended to alter decisions, revenue, or continuity of use.

**Decision.** Before launch, define the hypothesis, affected audience, desired outcome, risk of misunderstanding, and reversal condition. Evaluate the business outcome alongside abandonment, complaints, accessibility, cancellations, refunds, support load, and disproportionate impact on affected groups.

**Evidence.** There is a baseline, period, justified segmentation, record of limitations, and criterion that invalidates the change; signs of harm trigger correction, pause, or reversal.

**Avoid.** Declaring success only from immediate revenue, testing billing without transparency, treating reduced cancellation as proof of satisfaction, or retaining a pattern that increases support load, confusion, or lock-in.
