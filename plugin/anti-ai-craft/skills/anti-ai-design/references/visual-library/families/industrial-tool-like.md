# Industrial / tool-like

Purpose-built control surfaces whose geometry follows instruments, physical systems, media channels, or precise operational state.

**Usually appropriate for:** Audio, 3D, device control, deployment, machine-like configuration, and expert production tools.

**Frequent errors:** Imitating hardware cosmetically; obscuring current state; removing labels in the name of minimalism.

**Do not use by default for:** General content browsing, simple consumer forms, or domains without a stable control-and-feedback model.

**Products represented:** Blender, FreeCAD, GIMP, Kdenlive, KiCad, Nextcloud, OpenShot, darktable.
