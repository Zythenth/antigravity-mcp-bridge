## ADO-069 — Measure continuity by the value that remains

**Strength.** required

**Apply when.** The team uses renewal, login, session time, or account count as a signal that the product remains useful.

**Decision.** Define continuity from tasks or outcomes that still help the person, considering context, expected frequency, and the possibility of episodic use. Cross-check quantitative signals with support, research, and observation without inferring intent from a single event.

**Evidence.** The definition states the population, period, limitations, and difference among return, useful completion, and mere exposure; there is evidence that the signal relates to perceived value.

**Avoid.** Calling every return healthy retention, requiring frequent use of a naturally occasional product, collecting excessive telemetry, or turning surveillance into a continuity strategy.

## ADO-070 — Correct expectations before trying to retain

**Strength.** required

**Apply when.** People leave early, report surprise, request a feature they believed existed, or do not recognize the promised outcome.

**Decision.** Compare the proposition, demonstration, pricing, initial setup, and delivered experience; correct the gap closest to its origin. State limits and prerequisites before commitment, even when this reduces unsuitable conversion.

**Evidence.** Interviews, support messages, journey data, or tests show where expectations diverge; the correction is verified with people representing the affected context.

**Avoid.** Attributing exit to lack of engagement, compensating for an incorrect promise with discounts, blaming people for not understanding ambiguous language, or expanding marketing before correcting delivery.

## ADO-071 — Make staying a renewable choice

**Strength.** default

**Apply when.** The product depends on continued use, renewal, recurring collaboration, or reassessment of value over time.

**Decision.** Reaffirm value through comprehensible outcomes, reliability, relevant improvements, and the person's control over preferences. Design return because the task continues to make sense, not because leaving was made difficult.

**Evidence.** The person can identify recent value, adjust notifications and preferences, reduce use, or leave when necessary; research and behavior do not indicate induced dependency or confusion.

**Avoid.** An unpredictable reward mechanism to hold attention, persistent notifications, reactivation without consent, or confusing an exit barrier with delivered value.

## ADO-072 — Make accumulated work visible without withholding data

**Strength.** default

**Apply when.** The experience contains history, settings, collaborations, documents, automations, or knowledge created by the person or team.

**Decision.** Make this body of work comprehensible to support an informed decision, indicating origin, updates, and dependencies. Maintain proportionate access, export, and migration so continuity results from utility and trust, not the impossibility of leaving.

**Evidence.** The person can locate important items, understand what depends on them, and obtain a usable copy of their content within applicable technical and permission limits.

**Avoid.** Threatening loss that will not occur, hiding export paths, mixing first-party content with third-party data without distinction, or using history as an argument to block cancellation.

## ADO-073 — Offer proactive help at the point of friction

**Strength.** default

**Apply when.** The same question, error, abandonment, or support request repeatedly appears at one point in the journey.

**Decision.** Investigate the cause and provide contextual guidance, plain language, recovery, and a help route when the need appears. When recurrence indicates a structural failure, correct the interface or process instead of merely expanding support.

**Evidence.** The problem is identified through aggregated, representative evidence; the intervention reduces effort without hiding rules, requiring additional data, or shifting responsibility to the person.

**Avoid.** A chatbot as the only exit, an unrecoverable tip, an automatic response to a sensitive situation, collecting unnecessary content, or using support to mask an incomprehensible flow.

## ADO-074 — Distinguish natural completion from loss of value

**Strength.** heuristic

**Apply when.** A person stops using a product that solves a periodic task, finite project, seasonal need, or completed obligation.

**Decision.** Separate legitimate completion, context change, experience defect, and loss of value before proposing re-engagement. Offer return only when there is a new concrete reason, and allow the person to define contact preferences.

**Evidence.** The analysis classifies reasons with stated uncertainty, considers natural usage cycles, and verifies whether the return attempt produces perceived benefit, not merely message opens.

**Avoid.** Treating every exit as a failure, creating an artificial task to maintain activity, sending repeated campaigns after refusal, or using guilt to turn completion into continued use.

## ADO-075 — Make pause, downgrade, and cancellation equally discoverable

**Strength.** required

**Apply when.** A person can reduce spending, temporarily pause, remove a payment method, end a subscription, or close an account.

**Decision.** Make these paths available in the same environment where purchasing and management occur, with explicit language and accessible focus and operation. Present alternatives only as equivalent options, without making cancellation less visible or more laborious.

**Evidence.** Someone can locate and complete each action by keyboard, touch, and screen reader, without mandatory contact, artificial waiting, or repeated steps; outcomes are confirmed and recorded.

**Avoid.** Hiding cancellation in an FAQ, requiring a phone call, using a maze of screens, preselecting pause as though it were cancellation, or turning confirmation into another sales pitch.

## ADO-076 — Explain offboarding before carrying it out

**Strength.** required

**Apply when.** Cancellation, account closure, user removal, or plan downgrade changes access, collaboration, data, payments, or integrations.

**Decision.** Show the real consequences, dates, owners, preserved content, removed content, recoverable actions, and how to get help. Clearly distinguish reversible from irreversible decisions and request confirmation proportionate to the impact.

**Evidence.** The flow was tested with representative data, different permissions, shared content, a network error, and later return; messages are comprehensible without depending only on color or a modal.

**Avoid.** A generic warning, threatening loss to prevent exit, immediate deletion when a recovery period was promised, omitting the effect on collaborators, or repeated confirmation without new risk.

## ADO-077 — Enable export and return without retaliation

**Strength.** required

**Apply when.** A person ends use, requests a copy, migrates data, removes an account, or returns after a pause.

**Decision.** Provide a usable export, scope and format instructions, a permission-compatible recovery path, and the possibility of return without arbitrary loss of dignity, price, or access to one's own data. Protect other people's data and system secrets during the process.

**Evidence.** Files can be opened and understood outside the product, the export covers what was promised, and return was tested without recreating artificial administrative barriers.

**Avoid.** A deliberately useless format, partial export without warning, obstructing import into a competing product, charging to release one's own data, or reactivating billing without explicit authorization.

## ADO-078 — Learn from exit without coercion

**Strength.** default

**Apply when.** A person cancels, reduces use, does not renew, requests deletion, or completes a temporary journey.

**Decision.** Offer a short, optional, proportionate feedback request, prioritizing a free-text field or revisable categories; separate feedback collection from completion of offboarding. Use aggregated signals to investigate causes and communicate corrections only to those who consent to contact.

**Evidence.** Cancellation works without answering the survey, reasons are analyzed with context and uncertainty, and later changes can be related to an observable problem rather than a defensive interpretation.

**Avoid.** A mandatory questionnaire, an answer that reopens the flow, contact after opt-out, exposing the reason to people without a need to know, or treating individual feedback as universal truth.
